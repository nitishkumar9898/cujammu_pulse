# CUJAMMU-PULSE: Faculty, Volunteers and Configurable Attendance

Version 1.4 | 2 October 2026 | Normative target design, not deployed functionality or verified security.

Read with [UNIVERSITY_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md](UNIVERSITY_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md), [CORE_FEATURES_SPEC.md](CORE_FEATURES_SPEC.md), [SCHEDULING_AND_NOTIFICATION_SPEC.md](SCHEDULING_AND_NOTIFICATION_SPEC.md), [PLATFORM_PARITY_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md](PLATFORM_PARITY_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). CF-24 through CF-28 extend the previous scope. This document supersedes earlier event-specific assumptions that every second QR is an exit QR or every attendance policy requires 80% duration. Library Study/Book Issue entry/exit, hostel scans and their policies remain separate.

## 1. Faculty organizer and approval workspace (CF-24)

Faculty is a verified institutional person type; ORGANIZER is a separately scoped duty. Verified faculty can create/submit proposals through the assigned university workflow and become lead/co-organizer for their approved event. Student organizers and other authorized organizers use the same workflow; self-registration never grants organizer/admin authority. Own event management does not grant unrelated event exports, marksheets or scanner delegation across the university.

`Create Event -> Configure Sessions / Attendance -> Submit -> Assigned Admin Review -> Approve -> Event Operations Workspace`. Approval creates exactly one scoped workspace and volunteer-recruitment configuration through idempotent transaction/outbox. Workspace is an operations panel, not an extra attendee session or automatic public publication. Public event discovery follows the existing PUBLISHED policy; volunteer recruitment may open after APPROVED when its own window permits. A rejected/unapproved proposal cannot recruit through a guessed operations link or enable scanners.

Lead/co-organizers have explicit capabilities such as event.operations.manage, volunteer.recruit, volunteer.review, scanner.control, scanner.delegate, attendance.manual and attendance.export, constrained to their event/session and validity. Only a grant with delegation authority can grant eligible scanner/manual access; faculty title alone does not permit delegation. Required reapproval for material policy changes remains in force. Organizers cannot approve their own proposal by also selecting an admin persona.

## 2. Volunteer registration link and roster (CF-25)

After approval the organizer/faculty sees `[Open Volunteer Registration]`, `[Copy Recruitment Link]`, `[Close Registration]`, `[Review Volunteers]`, `[Accept]`, `[Reject]`, `[Assign Shift]`, `[Remove Volunteer]` and `[Rotate Link]`. Copy/share applies to the recruitment link only; attendee QR credentials have no share/download action. Sending the link from the product uses the approved notification channel and explicit chosen audience; copying a link does not silently email contacts.

Link opens the same authenticated form on Android and web. It identifies the event but is not a scanner capability or student-data access token. Link expiry, recruitment window, audience eligibility, bounded capacity, rate limits and optional hashed invite secret are enforced server-side. Secret rotation/closing disables old links for new applications without deleting accepted volunteers. Avoid exposing invite secrets to analytics, referrer logs or public search indexes. Showing a basic public event title does not expose applicant lists.

Student volunteer fields: verified full name, **roll number or enrollment number (at least one mandatory)**, verified department/program and university contact identity. Prefill from the authoritative student registry and validate that the identifier belongs to the authenticated subject; a typed roll number cannot create an application for another student. Capture both when officially available; preserve leading zeros and approved identifier spelling. Unmapped identities route to office verification, never an automatically accepted invented roll number. Personal phone/address is not mandatory by default. Consent/notice, availability and chosen duty are versioned; collect only justified details.

Volunteer application states: APPLIED -> ACCEPTED / REJECTED -> ASSIGNED -> COMPLETED, plus withdrawal/removal. Recruitment config can choose policy-approved automatic acceptance or reviewed acceptance; default reviewed. `[Allow All Volunteers]` means all currently accepted eligible roster members, including later accepted members while ALL remains enabled, never unreviewed applicants. Students are the default volunteer population; an exceptional staff operator uses a verified staff ID and distinct operator provisioning path, not a fabricated student identifier. Volunteer enrollment is independent of attendee registration/capacity; if a volunteer also attends, model both duties without auto-awarding attendee presence or volunteer hours.

Unique institution + event + subject volunteer application/membership; retries cannot duplicate a roster entry. Roster full/recruitment closed has explicit status. Export only the assigned organizer's necessary roster fields. Attendance, hours and certificate policies remain independent, supervisor-reviewed where required.

## 3. Attendance mode matrix (CF-26)

Attendance mode is frozen per approved session, allowing an event default with explicit session overrides. Configure before registration where possible; material changes later require preview, reapproval, revision and affected-user notification. Never reinterpret already accepted scans or silently erase evidence.

| Mode | Registration/pass | Organizer-controlled operation | Attendance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| SINGLE_QR | One credential family bound to confirmed registration, displayed as short-lived QR when opened | Open/close ENTRY gate and scanner master; choose ENTRY_ONLY or ENTRY_AND_ATTENDANCE before publish | ENTRY_ONLY records admission; ENTRY_AND_ATTENDANCE atomically records admission + point-in-time attendance from one accepted scan |
| DUAL_QR | First credential family is Entry Pass QR | Separate `[Enable Attendance QR]` during valid event/session window exposes a second distinct ATTENDANCE_CHECKPOINT family; scanner master/grants still required | Entry plus accepted second checkpoint; the second QR is not inherently an EXIT |
| NO_QR | Non-QR registration confirmation/pass reference; no QR minted | Organizer opens approved manual attendance window; selected manual delegates only | Reasoned verified manual entry/checkpoint/exit as required by published policy |

Both SINGLE_QR sub-policies are supported: organizer can use one QR for admission only or for admission and attendance. Entry-only does not silently mean certificate eligibility. Attendee sees the selected mode, required actions and certificate policy before registering. NO_QR does not mean automatically present or an unverified self-check-in.

DUAL_QR supports the user-requested entry + later attendance checkpoint workflow. Its checkpoint can prove verified presence at that moment, not an uninterrupted 80% stay. If duration-based eligibility is required, the organizer must select explicit **ENTRY_EXIT_DURATION** evidence policy and obtain authorized exit evidence (exit scan context or reviewed manual closure), or an approved richer interval/checkpoint policy. Label the screen `[Scan Exit]` only for a real EXIT action; the second enabled attendance QR must not be called Exit QR-2 unless that session explicitly uses it as EXIT under its published policy. Exit is an action context, not automatically an additional QR-count mode; credential family reuse for exit requires separately enabled authorized EXIT validation, never generic acceptance.

Canonical ledger actions: ENTRY, ATTENDANCE_CHECKPOINT, EXIT and MANUAL_CORRECTION; source QR / MANUAL / APPROVED_OFFLINE. Display states include REGISTERED, ONLY_ENTRY, ATTENDANCE_CONFIRMED, FULL_ATTENDANCE, EARLY_DEPARTURE, INCOMPLETE, ABSENT and UNDER_REVIEW. ATTENDANCE_CONFIRMED is point evidence. FULL_ATTENDANCE requires the published evidence/eligibility policy; duration >=80% applies only where that duration policy is approved and sufficient interval evidence exists. Certificate template/type truthfully distinguishes participation/checkpoint attendance from verified duration; no automatic duration inference from a single or second checkpoint scan.

### Second QR activation and audience notification

During an eligible live session organizer/faculty selects `[Enable Attendance QR]`, action context, bounded opening/closing times, scope and confirms. Commit a new attendance-window epoch/revision plus notification intent/audit. Before activation the second card reads `Not enabled yet`; after activation it appears on every currently confirmed eligible registrant's own dashboard with `Attendance QR enabled`, time remaining and instructions. Users who have not completed required entry see the card with `Entry required`; they cannot record checkpoint attendance remotely by receiving a notification. Waitlisted/cancelled/ineligible users cannot obtain a usable QR.

Registered users receive a durable notification plus permitted push/email attempts under CF-19 targets. Notification contains a safe link, not a QR image, secret or raw identifier. Online dashboards update through authenticated realtime and reconnect/catch-up. Lazy issuance on the own dashboard avoids precomputing/exporting thousands of secret tokens; activation makes the credential family available to all eligible registrants, while each display token is short-lived and individually bound. Later confirmed registrants get current window status; late registration/entry policy still applies.

Closing/reopening a window changes epoch, expires older display tokens and reminders, and preserves already accepted evidence. A repeated activation request with the same idempotency key does not create duplicate alerts; a genuine reopen is an audited new intent. Student/device time cannot extend the window. Enabling attendance QR does not enable volunteers' scanners; enabling scanners does not open an attendance window.

## 4. Scanner and manual delegation (CF-27)

Event/session scanner master defaults **OFF**, including after approval and roster acceptance. Attendance-window state, scanner master and recipient capabilities are separate controls. Organizer/faculty with scanner.control can enable/disable it only for their assigned valid session, set allowed ENTRY/CHECKPOINT/EXIT actions, time bounds and eligible operators. Organizers themselves require assigned scan capability plus the open master/window; being event owner does not bypass an intentionally closed scanner. NO_QR never enables the QR camera path.

`[Enable QR Scanning] -> [All Accepted Volunteers] or [Select Volunteers] -> Review exact current recipients/actions/window -> Confirm`. ALL is an explicit roster-bound policy: every current accepted eligible volunteer, including a newly accepted member while ALL remains enabled, receives only the configured QR scope. The confirmation says `Includes newly accepted volunteers while enabled`; roster acceptance and permission computation contend on the same control/membership versions and audit the effective access. An unreviewed applicant never qualifies. Show the current permitted count/list and notify new authorized operators. SELECTED grants cannot target outside-event users or expired/rejected/removed members; new members remain disabled until explicitly selected. ALL never grants manual or export authority.

Volunteer `Scan QR` control is disabled with a reason until all conditions hold: master ON, own effective grant, accepted roster membership, relevant action window, noncancelled lifecycle and authorized enrolled operator device/session. Once enabled it appears/activates identically on app/web; permission loss disables it promptly. Every scan API and realtime subscription rechecks these conditions server-side, so cached UI/direct requests cannot bypass OFF or selected-only rules. Revocation increments grant/control epoch, invalidates pending scan intents, terminates authorized channels as applicable and excludes removed members. Master OFF does not delete history.

Manual attendance is a **separate capability and manual window**, never automatically included with ALL or selected QR grants. Assigned organizer/faculty has their scoped default manual capability under policy; it still requires a valid explicit manual window. `[Grant Manual Attendance]` selects particular accepted volunteers, permitted actions/session, reason and expiry; `[Revoke Manual Access]` is immediate server authority. QR permission alone must fail manual endpoint checks; manual-only delegates do not gain QR scanner/export rights. No broad ALL shortcut for student manual delegation.

Turning scanning/manual access on/off is audited and notifies affected operators of their current scope; students are notified only for actionable attendance-window changes. Admin removal of organizer access, event cancellation/postponement and roster removal revoke dependent powers. All scanner operations are online by default for strong live revocation/anti-relay policy. Approved offline scanning is provisional with frozen manifests and explicit supervisor reconciliation; permission changes cannot reach a disconnected device instantly, and no offline evidence auto-finalizes certification.

## 5. QR privacy and anti-proxy controls

Every event QR credential is server-bound to institution, event/session, confirmed subject/registration, verified identifier record/version, credential family/action, schedule/policy/window epoch, nonce/jti and expiry. The roll/enrollment identifier is mandatory in the associated credential record and accepted receipt. Use an opaque signed reference resolved by the authorized server, rather than leaking plaintext roll numbers to anyone with a camera; if a reviewed offline design truly needs embedded identifiers, use a standard vetted encrypted envelope with only authorized enrolled scanner access. Never print credentials, tokens or student identifiers in public notifications, logs or QR URLs.

No `[Share QR]`, download-to-gallery, forwarding, printable attendance QR or publicly accessible pass URL. Apply private no-store responses, account-switch cleanup, no analytics token capture and screenshot/cast protection on supported native sensitive screens. Browser screens cannot universally prevent screenshots/external photos; visual deterrents are not security authority. [Android secure activity guidance](https://developer.android.com/security/fraud-prevention/activities?hl=en) describes native FLAG_SECURE; it cannot prevent another camera filming the display.

For the default high-assurance online flow, scanning creates a short-lived pending scan intent, not final attendance. Server challenges the credential owner's authenticated session to `[Confirm Scan]`, bound to operator, event/session, action, scan intent and expiry; validate fresh standard cryptographic/authenticated proof from that account. Reauthorize operator, lifecycle, epoch/window and registration at commit, then atomically consume the credential/action attempt and append decision/audit. An anonymous holder of a screenshot cannot complete this flow alone. No code emailed to a friend or client-supplied subject overrides ownership. Use standard WebAuthn user verification/appropriate native proof where required and supported, with documented supervised fallback; a passkey is authentication, not proof of physical location or necessarily one nonsynchronized device. [W3C WebAuthn](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-2/) specifies RP challenge assertions.

Supervised operator compares minimal authorized display identity/verified student ID to the person; short validity, live confirmation, device/session controls, replay detection and configured presence corroboration reduce relay risks. Colluding users can still relay live confirmations or lend devices; do not claim zero proxy risk or mathematically noncopyable QR. No facial-recognition collection or continuous location tracking is added. Unsupported proof, dead phone or network outage routes to explicitly authorized reviewed manual fallback, never an automatic weakened QR acceptance. NO_QR shares the same identity verification and audit expectations.

Unique receipt for institution + session + subject + action + window/interval attempt as appropriate. Re-entry/exit use an explicit presence-cycle sequence; repeated checkpoint scans in the same window return the stable receipt. Pending proof expires without attendance and does not consume admission; concurrent scanners cannot both finalize the same attempt. A copied QR scanned by the wrong principal cannot become that principal's attendance or overwrite the owner's identifier.

## 6. Manual roll/enrollment attendance

`[Manual Attendance] -> exact Roll/Enrollment lookup within current confirmed participant roster -> minimal matched identity -> Check official ID / record reason -> choose permitted action -> Confirm`. Missing QR/device switched off is a reason category, not a new event enrollment. No campus-wide student search, fuzzy disclosure of other profiles, caller-supplied name binding or arbitrary attendance insertion. A wrong/unmapped/ambiguous number fails safely; legitimate unregistered walk-in requires its separate eligibility/capacity admission workflow first.

Preserve original identifier strings and canonical person mapping; rate-limit enumeration. Record operator/grant, event/session, server time, observed time if bounded policy allows, reason, source MANUAL and any confidential evidence reference. Manual and QR duplicates converge on the same action/attendance invariants. Corrections are append-only reviewed records. Backdating/overrides require separate authority/reviewer; no volunteer can self-award attendance/hours/certificate. Where an operator is also an attendee, another authorized operator records their presence or an independent review approves the exception.

Dead-phone identity-confirmed attendance may be accepted under the published manual policy; clearly label evidence quality. High-assurance certificates require review/independent verification when the policy calls for it. Manual entry/checkpoint does not create missing exit time or a fabricated 80% duration. Reasoned fallback remains available to organizer/faculty and only their selected manual delegates on both clients.

## 7. Ascending identifier reports (CF-28)

Attendance rows always include verified roll/enrollment, name/department as authorized, stable participant/session reference, evidence source, accepted entry/checkpoint/exit times, policy/result status and quality. Export grants belong to event organizer/faculty or specifically authorized staff; ordinary QR/manual volunteers do not automatically get whole-roster exports. Never export live QR payloads, private contacts, proof signatures or unrestricted audit details.

`[Download Attendance CSV]` produces one final summary row per scoped registered participant/session, including absent/incomplete participants so missing scans cannot disappear. `[Download Scan Log CSV]` produces the authorized accepted QR/manual action ledger with stable scan ID, action/source/operator/time and linked verified identifier; rejected-attempt security details require a separate restricted audit export. Provide styled XLSX with the same result and identifiers as text. Both reports sort **ascending by approved roll/enrollment order**, then session ordering and accepted action time/scan ID. Live attendance table uses the same comparator; sorting is a query/projection, not rewriting append-only evidence.

Institution defines primary identifier (official roll where available, otherwise enrollment) and a versioned natural-order comparator: normalized approved prefix + numeric segments in numeric order + remaining suffix, with stable person ID tie-break. Example `CS-2 < CS-10`; preserve `0012` literally and never cast the whole identifier to integer. Numeric-only IDs sort numerically with original string/stable ID tie-break for equivalent numbers; leading zeros remain in exported values. For multiple departments/programs, an approved prefix/cohort grouping makes ordering unambiguous; show sort rule in report metadata. Missing/duplicate verified identifiers block finalization for office resolution, not invented row numbers. An officially nonstudent attendee uses verified staff/external registration ID under distinct participant type, never a fabricated student roll.

Server-side generated sort keys/comparator, indexed scope and deterministic snapshot pagination cover the **whole report**, not only the current screen page. CSV quoting/encoding and spreadsheet-formula defense are mandatory; CSV consumers may auto-convert numeric strings, so supply import-as-text guidance and the text-typed XLSX alternative. Generate with report revision, watermark/cutoff, policy/sort version, timezone, row count and checksum in metadata/manifest. Names/identifiers remain scoped personal data.

Finalization requires closed attendance windows and resolved pending proof/offline/review items or explicitly labelled unresolved status per approved policy. Later valid corrections create a new version, not silent replacement. Preview/in-progress exports are labelled provisional. Accepted scans are recorded immediately; report generation and live sorting do not wait to persist them. Notification on report READY/FAILED goes to assigned owners, not every registrant with a private roster attachment.

## 8. MongoDB, commands and observability

Add `eventOperations`, `volunteerRecruitment`, `volunteerApplications`, `volunteerMemberships`, `attendancePolicies`, `attendanceWindows`, `scannerControls`, `scannerGrants`, `manualAttendanceGrants`, `eventCredentialRecords`, `pendingScanIntents`, `attendanceDecisions` and report snapshot metadata. Existing person registry/registration/presence/outbox collections remain authority. Unique approved workspace, application and membership keys; bounded controls, membership/grant/window epochs; partial/compound unique accepted-action keys; validated identifier refs and derived sort keys. Do not store whole rosters or QR families in unbounded event arrays. Grant/control revoke-versus-scan must contend on the authoritative control aggregates in the same transaction, not rely on a stale lookup snapshot. Optimize contention only with a separately proven equivalent design; measure gate throughput with real replica-set tests.

Explicit APIs include `/events/{id}/operations`, `/events/{id}/volunteer-recruitment`, `/events/{id}/volunteer-applications`, `/sessions/{id}/attendance-policy`, `/sessions/{id}/attendance-windows`, `/sessions/{id}/scanner-control`, `/sessions/{id}/scanner-grants`, `/sessions/{id}/manual-grants`, `/me/registrations/{id}/credentials`, `/attendance/scan-intents`, `/attendance/scan-intents/{id}/confirm`, `/attendance/manual`, and scoped report commands. Capability checks include exact event/session/action, lifecycle/window/master/grant/membership epochs and independent manual/export authority. Mutations require version/idempotency; no unrestricted status/role fields. Credentials/proof references are never URL query tokens or public GET artifacts.

Monitor denied OFF/expired/revoked scans, duplicate attempts, incomplete proof, enrollment lookup abuse, identifier conflicts, manual proportion/corrections, grant drift, notification lag and provisional reports. Redact token/proof/identifier/contact data from telemetry; detailed investigation is private audited access. Alerts request human review, not automatic accusations or disciplinary decisions.

## 9. Shared UX, verification and implementation entry gate

Version 1.4 runtime policy is [RUNTIME_FEATURES_AND_MEDIA_SPEC.md](RUNTIME_FEATURES_AND_MEDIA_SPEC.md): institution admin may hide/disable operations, recruitment, QR/manual/export actions independently in compatible clients, with matching backend checks. Institution OFF overrides organizer event controls; re-enable does not reopen old attendance windows/grants. Uploaded evidence/media expires after 30 days with inherited derivative expiry; retain only permitted structured decisions/provenance and protected hold exceptions. This overrides blanket attachment/permanent-original retention assumptions.

Operations workspace tabs: Overview, Sessions & Attendance, Volunteer Recruitment, Roster & Permissions, Live Attendance, Notifications, Reports, Change History. Mode wizard shows exactly what each QR proves, certificate requirements and confirmation preview. Volunteer dashboard separates My Duties, Scan QR (with gate/action), Manual Attendance (only delegated) and My Hours. Student pass screen separates Entry Pass and Attendance QR, current mode/window/countdown and accepted receipts; no share controls. Camera success/haptic follows server-confirmed outcome; pending owner confirmation is visibly pending. Screen-reader status, keyboard flow, large touch controls and reduced motion are required on both clients.

Before implementing, trace CF-24..28 and updated CF-07/13 through shared actions/OpenAPI/state machines/collections/tests; resolve registry identifier/sort/approval/manual policy with institutional owners using synthetic fixtures. Prototype runtime review findings remain open until fixed and tested. Foundation work can start from this documented design, but do not claim build compatibility, security, native/web camera behavior, load SLOs or university production acceptance have already passed. No real records imported/deployment enabled by this document change.

Tests must cover faculty duty isolation, approval-workspace idempotency, recruitment links/duplicate roster/identifier mismatch, mode changes and proof sufficiency, second-window audience/reopen/notification retries, master OFF and roster-bound ALL/new accepted volunteers, selected-only/manual separation and live revocation, screenshot/replay/owner-proof abuse, cancelled/superseded/offline credentials, dead-phone/manual self-recording and QR/manual concurrency, report natural sorting across pages/leading zeros/formula attacks and native/web equivalent UI states.
